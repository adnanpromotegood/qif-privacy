Privacy Policy — Qif (قف)

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Qif (“we”, “our”, “the app”) is a mindfulness app developed by Promote Good Inc. This policy explains what Qif collects and what stays on your device.

Our Core Principle

Qif works mainly on your device. We collect as little as we can, never sell your information, and never link your app activity to your name or email.

What Stays on Your Device

Your guarded apps list, your lifetime dhikr counts, a local record of your pauses, and your settings are stored only on your phone, in Android’s local database and shared preferences. They never leave your device. If you uninstall Qif, they are permanently deleted.

What We Collect

(1) Your name and email address. You enter these once during setup and they are stored in Google’s Firebase Firestore. We use your email to contact you about Qif, such as important notices about the app or this policy. If you tick the box “Email me about new features and updates”, we may also send you news about features and updates, and you can unsubscribe at any time. We store whether you ticked the box, when, and which version of the wording you saw.

(2) Anonymous pause events. Each time you finish or leave the pause screen, Qif records one event with: what you did (walk away, open the app, or dismiss), the time and hour of day, how long the pause lasted, whether it was triggered by opening the app or by a time limit, whether a dhikr or a verse was shown and which one, how many dhikr you tapped, the package name of the app you opened (for example com.instagram.android) and its broad category (for example social), your app language, and your platform. Events contain no name, email, account, device identifier or advertising ID, and are not linked to you. We use them to understand how Qif is used, to improve it, and to tell people about it.

What We Do Not Collect

Your messages, screen content, passwords, what you type, photos, contacts, files or location. Qif contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Accessibility Service

Qif uses Android’s Accessibility Service to detect when you open an app you have chosen to guard, so it can show the pause screen. It listens only for app-switch events (TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED) and is configured so it cannot retrieve screen content. It does not read your screen, messages, passwords or what you type. The only information it passes on is the name of the app that was opened, which is used on your device and recorded in the anonymous pause events described above.

Usage Access

Qif uses Usage Access to confirm which app is in the foreground at the moment of the pause. This is used only on your device to decide when to show the pause.

Display Over Other Apps

Qif uses this permission to show the pause screen on top of the app you opened. It does not monitor or interact with other apps.

Subscriptions and Payments

Qif is free. You can optionally support Qif through a subscription processed entirely by Google Play. We do not collect, store or have access to your payment information.

Third-Party Services

Firebase Firestore (Google) stores the data described above, in Google’s data centers in Europe. As with any online service, Google receives standard technical information, such as your IP address, when your phone connects; we do not use it to identify you. Google Play Billing handles optional payments. Both are covered by Google’s privacy policy. If we send email to people who have opted in, we may use an email service provider acting on our behalf.

Your Choices and Rights

You can ask us to delete or correct your name and email, or tell us you no longer want emails, by contacting support@promotegood.ca. We will respond within 30 days. Because pause events are not linked to you, we cannot identify or delete your individual events. Local data is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Data Retention

We keep your name and email until you ask us to delete them. We keep anonymous pause events for as long as they are useful for understanding how the app is used.

Children’s Privacy

Qif is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time and will change the date above. For significant changes we will notify users in the app or by email.
