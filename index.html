Privacy Policy — Qif (قف)

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Qif (“we”, “our”, “the app”) is a mindfulness app developed by Promote Good Inc. This privacy policy explains how Qif handles your information.

Our Core Principle

Qif is designed to work primarily on your device. We collect as little data as possible and never sell your information to anyone.

What Data Stays on Your Device

Your guarded apps list, dhikr counts, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your phone using Android’s local database and shared preferences. This data never leaves your device. If you uninstall Qif, this data is permanently deleted.

What Data We Collect

If you choose to provide it during setup, we collect your name and email address, stored securely in Firebase Firestore. We also collect anonymous, non-identifiable event data when you interact with the gate screen (e.g., walk-away vs open-app counts). This data contains no personal identifiers and is used solely to understand how the app is used in aggregate.

Accessibility Service

Qif uses Android’s Accessibility Service solely to detect when you open a guarded app. It only listens for app-launch events (TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED). It does NOT read, collect, or transmit any screen content, text, passwords, messages, or personal data from any app. It cannot see what you type, read, or view.

Subscriptions and Payments

Qif subscriptions are processed entirely through Google Play. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment information.

Third-Party Services

Firebase Firestore (data storage), Google Play Billing (payments). Both governed by Google’s privacy policy.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local data is deleted when you uninstall the app. To request deletion of your email and name from our servers, contact adnan@promotegood.ca. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Children’s Privacy

Qif is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
